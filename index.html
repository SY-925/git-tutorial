Research Directions

My primary research interests:

⚡

Speculative Sampling

Accelerating LLM inference using a small draft model to propose candidate tokens, verified in parallel by the target model. Achieves 2-3x speedup with identical output distributions.

🧠

Knowledge Distillation

Compressing large teacher models into compact students by transferring soft-label distributions and intermediate representations. Building efficient draft models for speculative decoding.

🔗

Draft Model Optimization

Using KD to produce better draft models for speculative sampling, where improved acceptance rate directly boosts decoding throughput.

🚀

Efficient LLM Serving

Applying speculative sampling and distillation to production serving systems, reducing latency and compute costs while preserving quality.
